My Design Thinking Approach

Every project starts with people — understanding their goals, frustrations, and context. From there I define the real problem, explore solutions, prototype the experience, and refine through feedback.

01

Empathize

Understand users through research, interviews, and observation

02

Define

Frame the core problem and align on clear user goals

03

Ideate

Explore solutions, flows, and design directions

04

Prototype

Build wireframes and high-fidelity interactive designs

05

Test

Validate with users and iterate based on feedback
