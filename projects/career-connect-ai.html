UX/UI case study

CareerConnect AI

Job Search & Career Discovery Platform

An AI-powered job search experience designed to help job seekers discover relevant opportunities, manage applications, and navigate their job search with less friction.

My RoleUI/UX Designer
Timeline2 Months
ToolsFigma, FigJam, Balsamiq, Uizard, Maze
PlatformWeb / Mobile experience

01 / Overview

Making the job search feel more manageable.

CareerConnect AI brings job discovery, profile building, resume creation, mentoring and application tracking into one connected experience. I worked across research, structure and interface design to make the journey clearer and less repetitive.

02 / The problem

Job searching is already a demanding process.

Problem statement

Job seekers need a clearer way to find relevant opportunities and move through applications without repeated effort or uncertainty.

  • Finding relevant jobs can be time-consuming.
  • Important job details are not always visible before applying.
  • Repeated profile fields make applications feel longer.
  • Application status and job freshness can be unclear.

10 / Usability testing

Testing the moments that matter.

Maze usability testing focused on whether users could find relevant jobs, understand job details and recognize application progress. Feedback pointed to the need for clearer status cues, more visible job context and a shorter profile journey.

TestedDiscovery, profile completion and application tracking.
HeardUsers needed more context and confidence before applying.
ChangedClarified information hierarchy, confirmation and progress.

11 / Design improvements

Small changes that reduce friction.

Before

Profile completion felt long and repetitive.

After

Progress and existing profile information support a faster journey.

Before

Users were unsure whether an application was submitted.

After

Clearer confirmation and application tracking improve confidence.

From searching to applying with greater clarity.

CareerConnect AI brings job discovery, application management and career exploration into a more structured experience, helping users move from searching to applying with greater clarity.
