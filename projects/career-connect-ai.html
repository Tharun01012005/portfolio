Mobile UX/UI design case study

CareerConnect AI

A career and job application platform designed to make job discovery, profile completion, applications and career guidance simpler and more efficient.

RoleUX Research + UX/UI Design
FocusMobile product experience
ProcessResearch to high-fidelity UI

01 / Overview

Making the job search feel more manageable.

CareerConnect AI brings job discovery, profile building, resume creation, mentoring and application tracking into one connected mobile experience.

The challenge

Working professionals can struggle to discover relevant jobs and complete applications efficiently when profile setup is time-consuming, information is repeated, job freshness is unclear and application status is not immediately obvious.

My contribution

UX Research, User Research, Affinity Mapping, Persona Development, Competitive Research, Information Architecture, User Flow, Wireframing, UI Design, Design System and Prototype.

How might we?

How might we make job searching and applying faster, clearer and less repetitive while still helping users make confident career decisions?

02 / Research

Evidence before interface.

I conducted user research to understand frustrations and expectations around job searching, profile completion and applications.

Research sample: 28 responses. These are research findings, not product success metrics.

86%wanted to view all profile sections before starting completion.
86%had been confused about whether an application was submitted successfully.
61%had tried to apply for an expired job.
54%had stopped completing a profile because it felt too long or complicated.
68%mentioned re-entering information as a frustration.
57%mentioned too many profile fields.
86%expected salary and job description information.
79%preferred auto-filled profile information.

Other information users expected

  • 79% expected location information.
  • 75% expected company information.
  • Users wanted important context before applying.
  • Faster applications depended on less repeated entry.

03 / Understanding users

Two working-professional perspectives.

Two working-professional personas helped frame the main needs around faster job discovery, simpler profile completion and clearer application tracking.

Kannan

Actively searching for better career opportunities and relevant jobs.

  • Needs a simple profile setup and progress visibility.
  • Wants better recommendations and minimal repeated entry.
  • Frustrated by too many fields and a long completion process.

Sabarivasan

Looking for suitable full-time opportunities through a clear, user-friendly platform.

  • Needs clear job information and application updates.
  • Wants smooth navigation and efficient applications.
  • Frustrated by expired jobs and confusing profile setup.

04 / Affinity mapping

From responses to themes.

I grouped research responses into Goals, Needs and Frustrations to identify recurring patterns and prioritize the main UX problems.

05 / Competitive research

Learning from familiar job platforms.

Indeed

  • Strong job discovery
  • Detailed job information
  • Preference management
  • Saved jobs and profile management

Naukri

  • Profile completion and recommendations
  • Detailed profile structure
  • AI-assisted multiple applications
  • Resume and career guidance features

What this revealed

These platforms helped identify opportunities to simplify profile setup, improve job information visibility, clarify application status and reduce repeated effort.

06 / Information architecture

Organizing the product around user goals.

The information architecture organized the product around the user's core goals: discovering jobs, applying, building a resume, tracking applications and getting career guidance.

07 / User flow

Connecting the main journeys.

The user flow maps the journey from onboarding and authentication through job discovery, application, resume building, mentor booking and application tracking.

10 / From research to design

Every important decision had a reason.

Insight

Profile completion felt long and repetitive.

Design response

Simplified the profile journey and made progress easier to understand.

Insight

Users were unsure whether an application was successfully submitted.

Design response

Introduced clearer application confirmation and status tracking.

Insight

Expired jobs reduced confidence in search results.

Design response

Improved active-job clarity and surfaced important job information before applying.

Insight

Users disliked repeatedly entering the same information.

Design response

Used existing profile information to support a faster application experience.

Insight

Users wanted important job details before applying.

Design response

Prioritized salary, location, work mode, company information and job description.

12 / Outcome

Designing a clearer job-search experience.

  • Structured the job-search journey around real user needs.
  • Simplified the intended profile and application experience.
  • Improved clarity around job information and application tracking.
  • Created a consistent visual system across the product.
  • Developed the experience from research through high-fidelity UI.
