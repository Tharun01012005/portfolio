House of Tharagai

An elegant e-commerce experience for fashion accessories — optimized product browsing, cart flow, and checkout usability with fully responsive design.

RoleFrontend Developer
PlatformWeb (Responsive)
ToolsFigma, HTML, CSS
FocusDesign + Frontend

Overview

House of Tharagai is a fashion accessories brand offering gold-toned jewellery and everyday elegance pieces. I developed the frontend and created the design using my own creativity — from visual direction to responsive implementation.

Challenge

Create a premium shopping experience that reflects the brand's soft feminine identity while ensuring seamless product discovery, cart management, and checkout across all devices.

Contributions

  • Designed and developed the full frontend experience
  • Created original visual design and brand-aligned UI
  • Built responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile
  • Optimized product browsing, cart, and checkout flows

Key Features

  • Hero with brand messaging and dual CTAs
  • Curated product grid with filters and badges
  • Cart with quantity controls and order summary
  • Consistent navigation and brand presence
  • Fully responsive across breakpoints
